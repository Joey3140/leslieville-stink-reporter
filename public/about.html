Why a stench tracker? Because the city's not counting.

The Ashbridges Bay Wastewater Treatment Plant has served Toronto since 1910. It treats roughly half the city's sewage. For years, on the wrong wind, the east end has known about it before the wastewater even leaves the headworks.

How the data is collected

Reports are submitted by neighbours through this site. We don't run sensors. We're not a regulator. We're a public counter — when 40 people in M4M report rotten eggs in the same hour, that's a number the plant operators and the councillor's office can be asked about.

How the data is protected

We don't store IP addresses (only an HMAC hash for rate limiting, auto-deleted after 30 days). We don't ask for your name. Precise location is opt-in and jittered ~100m before storage. Notes are reviewed before they go live so the feed stays useful and not weird.

  • No login, no account, no name collected.
  • Rate limit: 10 reports per hour per IP. Cloudflare Turnstile verifies you're human.
  • Public stats show report count AND unique reporter count — so a single person filing 50 reports doesn't distort the picture.
  • Aggregated daily counts per FSA are kept indefinitely as a public record.

Who's behind this

A handful of east-end residents, no funding, no affiliation with the City of Toronto or Toronto Water. We forward an aggregate weekly summary to Coun. Paula Fletcher's office (Ward 14, Toronto–Danforth) every Monday morning. They have not asked us to do this. We do it anyway.

Also worth doing

This site is unofficial. Reports here are not automatically forwarded to the city. If you want a service request on file, please file with 311 as well.
